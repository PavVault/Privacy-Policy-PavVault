PavVault Track your product warranties. Never miss an expiration date again. PavVault is a personal organizer for product warranties, receipts, and important documents. Snap a photo of your receipt, add a few details about your purchase, and PavVault keeps track of everything — so when something breaks, you know exactly what's covered and where your paperwork is. What PavVault does Track every product's warranty in one place — name, shop, purchase date, price, and warranty length Attach receipts, warranty cards, invoices, and manuals as photos or PDFs Get reminders before a warranty expires, so you never miss your window for a repair or replacement Organize products by category Optional PIN and biometric protection Optional Cloud Backup to your own Google Drive, free for everyone Your data stays yours By default, everything is stored locally on your device. Cloud Backup, if you choose to use it, stores a single backup file in a private area of your own Google Drive — PavVault never sees or touches any other files in your account, and nothing is ever uploaded to a server we control.
