PavVault Privacy Policy

Effective: August 2026

Introduction

PavVault respects your privacy and is designed to help you organize and manage your product warranties, receipts and important documents. This policy explains how your information is stored and handled when using the app.

Information Stored

PavVault may store information you enter, including:

  • Product names
  • Store or seller names
  • Purchase dates
  • Warranty expiration dates
  • Product categories
  • Product prices
  • Receipt images
  • Warranty documents

If you sign in with Google to use Cloud Backup, PavVault also accesses basic Google account information (such as your email address) to identify you for that feature.

Data Storage

By default, your information is stored locally on your device.

If you choose Cloud Backup, a single backup file is stored in a private, app-specific area of your own Google Drive. This area is not visible in your regular Drive folders and PavVault cannot access any other files in your Drive — only the backup file it creates itself.

PavVault does not upload, sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties, other than Google, and solely to provide Cloud Backup if you choose to use it.

PavVault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Receipt Images and Documents

Documents you add remain private and are stored to help you manage your own warranties.

You are responsible for ensuring you have the legal right to store any documents you add.

Notifications

Warranty reminders are generated locally on your device to inform you about upcoming warranty expirations.

Data Security

PavVault offers optional PIN protection to help prevent unauthorized access.

PavVault also supports optional biometric unlock (fingerprint or face recognition), processed entirely by your device's operating system. PavVault never receives, stores, or has access to your biometric data — only a confirmation of whether authentication succeeded.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device, keeping your PIN secure, and keeping your Google account secure if using Cloud Backup.

Data Loss and Deletion

You are responsible for maintaining backups of your own information. PavVault is not responsible for data loss caused by device damage, device replacement, accidental deletion, operating system issues, revoked Google account access, or other circumstances outside reasonable control.

You may delete your local data anytime by clearing data in the app or uninstalling it. You may remove your Cloud Backup from within PavVault (Settings → Delete Cloud Backup), or by revoking PavVault's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Third Party Services

PavVault uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API solely to provide the optional Cloud Backup feature, if and when you choose to use it.

If future features require other third-party services, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Your Rights

You may delete your stored information at any time by removing data from the app, uninstalling it, or revoking Google account access as described above.
